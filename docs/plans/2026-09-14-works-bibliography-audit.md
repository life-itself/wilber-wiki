# Works bibliography audit

Audit date: 2026-09-14. Bead: `wilberwiki-at6.1`.

Scope: the 39 existing work pages and their index rows, plus the missing 2018
retitling *Integral Buddhism*. Result: 40 title/edition pages, **not 40 independent
books**. Forewords, articles, other recordings and pseudonymous collaborations are
not comprehensively inventoried (see `wilberwiki-at6.6` for collaborative additions).

## Method and confidence

Every entry below has a bibliographic outcome. “Corroborated” means the title, year,
and broad role/format match the cited catalog; it does not certify every assertion
in its existing long overview. F is an author-affiliated finding aid, not a substitute
for a copyright page. I is a secondary bibliography. Both contain ambiguities; the
Eye to Eye and Starter Kit cases demonstrate why we distinguish stronger evidence.

For ordinary entries, dates are original publication years; selected reprint dates
are noted only when established. “Not compared” means no edition-by-edition textual
comparison was performed. Existing `status` describes the page description, not a
guarantee that every edition or contributor has been exhaustively researched.

Sources:

## Guide evidence and editorial decisions

The phases guide uses the local Integral Psychology source, chapter 9 note 15
(around line 2811), for Wilber's own definitions of phases I–IV and his explicit
placement of that book in IV. Chapter 8 note 39 and chapter 12 note 12 establish
earlier differentiation of structures/states; avoid a false 2006 origin claim.
SES revised-edition discussion around lines 394 and 440 supports the historical
trilogy plans. No public link to either full-text source was added.

The main reading route is an editorial choice based on the distinct roles of SES
and IP in the local books, plus the publisher guide and Rufus's existing notes on
Integral Spirituality. The [publisher reader guide](https://www.shambhala.com/integral-theory-primer-a-readers-guide-to-ken-wilber-by-ali-akalin/)
supports the introductory roles of Brief History, Theory of Everything, and Integral
Vision. The new annotations do not claim an exhaustive contents comparison for
books not available here. Retitling relationships are documented; revision magnitude
remains unmeasured. The guides intentionally recommend optional branches rather
than declaring every later application redundant.
