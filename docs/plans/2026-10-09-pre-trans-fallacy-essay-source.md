## "First" — what can and can't be claimed

- The 1980 *ReVision* article is the earliest standalone essay with this title found in the bibliographies checked.
- *The Atman Project* (1980) also draws the prepersonal/transpersonal distinction and names the confusion the "pre/trans fallacy", according to [Kazlev's summary of Wilber phase 2](https://malankazlev.com/kheper/topics/Wilber/Wilber_II.html). That summary is secondary, and the book is not in `library/`. Which of the two came out first in 1980 was **not** established. Wiki copy should therefore say the essay "introduced the term in essay form in 1980", not that it was the first use of the concept.
- *SES* itself (ch. 6) says only "Ever since I began writing on the distinctions between prerational (or prepersonal) states of awareness and transrational (or transpersonal) states—what I called the pre/trans fallacy…", with no citation (`library/1995-sex-ecology-spirituality-full-text.md`, line 2549).

## Archiving assessment

No accessible copy was found. ReVision 1980 is print-only and not in Crossref or the Internet Archive full-text search. The 1982 *JHP* version is paywalled at SAGE. The most practical copy would be the *Eye to Eye* chapter (any edition) or *Collected Works* vol. 3, converted the same way as the SES and IP imports. That needs the site owner's copy and the same explicit go-ahead those imports had. Until a copy is obtained, `concepts/pre-trans-fallacy.md` keeps its SES-only excerpts. The page now carries only a bibliographic origin note, with no quotations from the essay.
