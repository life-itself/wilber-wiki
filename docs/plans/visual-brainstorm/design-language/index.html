The Pre/Trans Fallacy

One idea: before reason and beyond it both look nonrational, so each gets mistaken for the other. Three tiers; the two nonrational ones share the tint; the two errors are the only accent marks, and each skips the rational tier. The grammar, and what is ours

Credit: the typography, drawing rules, layout, exports and checks are the Seeds of Renaissance diagram system (Life Itself), applied as written; text is set as outlines with its outline.py approach so the SVGs render without fonts. The full table of what we follow and where we differ is in the spec; in short:

FollowApfel Grotezk Regular and Mittel, no bold; SoR's type scale at 1600 px; 4 px lines for what matters, 2 px for scaffolding, round caps, solid arrowheads, no dashes; panels in the tint with no outline and 40 px padding; labels on the thing named, no legends; title and grey subtitle top left, source bottom left, signature bottom right, 72 px margin; tight SVG for the page and padded PNG for sharing; must read at 400 px and in black and white.

Why rust: the site already uses an amber family (the roadmap star, #d97706); a warm red-brown with a cream tint reads as book paper and ink, prints well in greyscale, and keeps 5.5:1 as text on white. The two renders above in SoR red are there so the choice can be made by eye.

Open questions for Rufus

  1. Accent: rust and cream, or SoR red and pink? Both are rendered above for each piece.
  2. Quotations off the canvas: the new figures carry labels and a source line only; Wilber's sentences live on the page beneath. Right for the series, or should one short verbatim line be allowed on a canvas where it is the point?
  3. The pre/trans tiers: the rational tier is open (no panel) between the two tinted ones. Does that read as "seen as what it is", or as a missing box?
  4. The empty lattice: the cells are open because no stage-by-state example has been quote-verified. Fill them later with Wilber's examples, or keep the lattice open as a matter of style?
  5. Rung names: IS's Gebser list (archaic … integral) is used on the 2000 ladder as well, for shared words; IP's chart 1 names differ (Formalism, Pluralistic-relativism, Holistic-integralism, two mythic rows). Acceptable approximation, or show chart-1 names on the ladder?
  6. Signature length: mark plus three parts is about 620 px at 1600; drop wilber.wiki from the line, or keep it?
  7. Animation: fades and one drawn arrow per piece, 2.4 s a step. Quiet enough, or still too much for the site?
