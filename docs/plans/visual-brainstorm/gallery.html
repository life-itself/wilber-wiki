Twelve ways to draw Wilber

Options for posters, explainer diagrams, explorables and share images for the Wilber Wiki. Every direction rests on material already in the repo (concept pages, charts/, works/, the full texts in library/). Sketches are rough on purpose: they show the look and the mechanism, not the finish. Two directions, the pre/trans fallacy and states off the ladder, have been taken further: open the pre/trans poster and the states-off-the-ladder explorable.

01 · poster / share card

One thought, four windows

Take Wilber's own example, a single thought, and walk it round the four quadrants. The grid is familiar; the trick is to fill it with one concrete holon instead of abstract labels.

Shows
The four irreducible aspects of one holon, with the validity claim that belongs to each; optionally a flip to the eight zones.
Audience
Newcomer on the homepage or concept page; strong Open Graph candidate for concepts/four-quadrants.
Sources
concepts/four-quadrants.md; worked example in SES ch. 4 ("For example, I have a thought; a thought occurs to me… in the Upper-Right quadrant, there is a change in brain physiology"); eight zones in IS ch. 1 (library/2006-integral-spirituality-full-text.md).
Faithfulness
Wilber's structure The grid and its labels are his. The four example lines are drawn from his own worked example; the validity-claim labels are his terms (truthfulness, truth, justness, functional fit).
Effort
S static; M with the zone flip.
Risks
The most-drawn diagram in integral circles, so it must earn attention through the concrete example rather than the grid. Must not reproduce the book's own figure (the book figure lists developmental sequences in each quadrant; we show one holon instead).
02 · explainer diagram, three states

The Big Three become the Big One

Fused, differentiated, dissociated: Wilber's whole account of modernity's dignity and disaster in three frames, ending in flatland.

Shows
The difference between differentiation (good) and dissociation (bad), and why "flatland" is the collapse of I and We into It rather than a general complaint about science.
Audience
Reader of concepts/flatland, concepts/big-three, concepts/dignity-and-disaster-of-modernity; good slide for teaching.
Sources
concepts/big-three.md, concepts/flatland.md, concepts/dignity-and-disaster-of-modernity.md; SES ch. 4 ("I will refer to these as the Big Three"), ch. 11 ("What modernity differentiated, postmodernity must integrate"), ch. 12 ("the Big Three were reduced to the Big One"); Three Jewels mapping SES ch. 4.
Faithfulness
Wilber's structure The fused → differentiated → dissociated sequence and the "Big One" phrase are his. Interpretation Rendering "fused" as overlapping circles and "dissociated" as a slab with flattened lines is our pictorial choice; label it as such.
Effort
S
Risks
Easy to make it read as "science bad". Frame two must be visibly the hero (he calls differentiation the dignity of modernity). The premodern frame simplifies a lot of history into one dome.
03 · wall poster

The Great Nest, with depth and span

Concentric spheres everyone has seen, plus the part nobody draws: each level is deeper and rarer than the one it embraces.

Shows
The matter → body → mind → soul → spirit nest; Spirit as both highest level and the ground of all levels; the depth/span asymmetry that makes the "higher" also the rarer and more fragile.
Audience
Wall poster; teaching slide; header image for concepts/great-chain-of-being and concepts/holarchy.
Sources
concepts/great-chain-of-being.md, concepts/holarchy.md (tenets 7–8); IP ch. 1 ("ranging from matter to body to mind to soul to spirit… Spirit is thus both the very highest wave… and the ever-present ground of all the waves"); SES ch. 2 tenet 8 ("Each successive level of evolution produces GREATER depth and LESS span").
Faithfulness
Wilber's structure Concentric spheres are how he says the Great Nest should be pictured; "the paper itself represents immanent Spirit" is his own convention for the figure. Interpretation Putting depth/span (stated for atoms/molecules/cells) beside the matter–spirit nest joins two passages; the poster should say the span argument is made for physical holons.
Effort
M
Risks
Concentric circles plus five labels is close to the IP figure 1; keep our drawing clearly our own (typography, the inset, no copied layout). By IS (2006) Wilber narrows the Nest to a map of the Upper-Left quadrant, so the poster should date itself.
04 · interactive explorable

Pull a rung out

Wilber's own thought experiment for telling higher from lower: destroy a type of holon and see what else goes. Click a level and watch.

Shows
Tenet 9 as a thing you can do, which teaches tenets 4–5 (nested, not vice versa; transcend and include) as a side effect.
Audience
Newcomer who thinks "hierarchy" means ranking people; reader of concepts/holarchy or concepts/dominator-vs-growth-hierarchy.
Sources
concepts/holons.md, concepts/holarchy.md; SES ch. 2 tenets 4, 5, 9 ("Destroy any type of holon, and you will destroy all of the holons above it and none of the holons below it"; the "thought experiment" and the atoms–molecules–cells sequence are his).
Faithfulness
Wilber's structure The test, the series and the conclusion are his; the interaction simply performs the thought experiment he asks the reader to run.
Effort
M small JS, needs a reduced-motion path and a no-JS fallback (the static "molecules removed" frame).
Risks
Physics pedants: "destroying all molecules" is not a coherent physical event; it's a logical test and the copy must say so. Keep it to types, as he does.
05 · single-idea explainer · poster · share card

The pre/trans fallacy, in one picture

Three bands of development, an eye that can only see two, and the two mistakes that follow. Freud pulls down; Jung lifts up.

Shows
Why pre and trans get confused (both are nonrational), and the two symmetrical errors. One picture, one idea; nothing to click.
Audience
Newcomer: this is the Wilber idea most likely to click first because it names a mistake people already half-recognise. Share card; teaching slide; header for concepts/pre-trans-fallacy.
Sources
concepts/pre-trans-fallacy.md; SES ch. 6 ("since both prerational states and transrational states are, in their own ways, nonrational, they appear similar or even identical to the untutored eye"; "Freud was a reductionist, Jung an elevationist"); SES ch. 14 note ("pre-ego to ego to trans-ego"); essay history in docs/plans/2026-10-09-pre-trans-fallacy-essay-source.md.
Faithfulness
Wilber's structure The three bands, the "untutored eye", the two errors and the Freud/Jung pairing are all his. Interpretation Drawing the eye's view as two bins is our device for his sentence about resemblance.
Effort
S (built)
Risks
Band examples (infantile, magical, mythic / mystical, contemplative) must stay at the level of his own descriptions, not our additions. The essay itself (1980) isn't in library/, so all quotes come from SES.
06 · interactive explorable

Your own psychograph

Five sliders, one jagged profile. The fastest way to feel what "levels and lines" means is to make a person who is high in one and low in another.

Shows
Levels vs lines; why "what stage is this person at?" is the wrong question; where "stick points" would show up.
Audience
Reader of concepts/integral-psychograph and concepts/waves-and-streams; coaches and teachers.
Sources
concepts/integral-psychograph.md; IP ch. 2 ("The levels in the Great Nest are shown on the vertical axis, and through those levels run the various lines… I give five as examples: cognitive, moral, interpersonal, spiritual, and affective"); IP ch. 3 ("relatively independent").
Faithfulness
Wilber's structure Axes, the five example lines and the point are his. We redraw rather than trace his figure 2.
Effort
M
Risks
It will be read as a self-assessment quiz. It isn't one, and the page must say the heights are illustrative, not measured; Wilber also says much within a level is heterarchical and not captured by the chart. Keep it a toy that teaches a distinction, not a diagnostic.
07 · "what changed" diagram · interactive

Taking states off the ladder

In 2000 the mystical states sat on top of the developmental ladder. In 2006 they slid off it to become a second axis. One animation shows the single most-misunderstood change in Wilber's thought.

Shows
The difference between a structure-stage and a state; why a meditator at an early stage can have a profound state experience; why the "ladder" picture misled readers, and Wilber.
Audience
Anyone who has heard "Wilber ranks people". Reader of concepts/from-ses-to-integral-spirituality, works/phases (IV → V), concepts/psychic-subtle-causal-nondual.
Sources
IP ch. 1 ("the major states of gross, subtle, causal, and nondual are available to human beings at virtually any stage of growth… we can create a grid"); charts/chart-1-wilber-correlations.md (psychic/subtle/causal/nondual as the top of the structure spine; worldview column for the lower rungs); IS ch. 4 (Wilber-Combs lattice: "put those sequentially developing structure-stages… running up the left side of the grid… and then put the major states across the top (gross, subtle, causal, nondual)"); concepts/from-ses-to-integral-spirituality.md.
Faithfulness
Wilber's structure Both pictures are his: the IP spine with transpersonal structures on top, and the IS lattice. Interpretation Showing it as one animation (rungs become columns) is our framing of his own "no longer stacked on top… alongside". The caveat that IP already said this must stay visible.
Effort
M (built)
Risks
Row vocabulary differs between the books (IP basic structures vs IS's Gebser-like list); we use IP's worldview column to bridge, and must say so. Cells must stay empty or verbatim; no invented "mythic × subtle = vision of Jesus" content unless quoted.
08 · timeline poster

Wilber I–V: the books on a line

Thirty-odd books on one axis, grouped into the five phases Wilber and his commentators use, with the one-line "what changed" per phase and the unfinished trilogy drawn as what it is: unfinished.

Shows
Chronology, phase membership, the gap years, the trilogy that didn't happen. Not: influence, derivation or concept lineage.
Audience
Reader choosing what to read; works/phases and works/index header image; a printable poster for a reading group.
Sources
works/phases.md (the five phases, "Why the boundaries are approximate", Kosmos trilogy status), works/catalog.md (dates, formats, editions), docs/plans/2026-09-14-works-bibliography-audit.md (uncertainty ledger), assets/covers/.
Faithfulness
Repo data for dates. Interpretation for phase bands (author retrospection plus commentators; phases.md notes Helfrich and Integral World date them differently). Bands must be drawn soft-edged and labelled approximate.
Effort
M
Risks
This is the safe, sourced half of what wilberwiki-at6.4 wanted. Adding concept arrows between books would reintroduce every problem the at6.4 review flagged. Resist. Anthologies and retitled editions need a distinct mark so they don't read as new works.
09 · data graphic for the people index

Who lives where in SES

The twenty-three interlocutors of Sex, Ecology, Spirituality, placed by the chapter where Wilber argues with them most, sized by how often. A map of the book's conversations, from data the wiki already has.

Shows
The book's argument structure through its interlocutors: the systems theorists up front, the developmentalists in ch. 6, Plotinus in Book Two, the postmoderns at the end. Nothing about "schools" or quadrant placement.
Audience
Reader of people/index; someone deciding which chapter of SES to read for a given thinker.
Sources
people/index.md (mention counts and peak chapters, already computed), people/*.md, library/1995-sex-ecology-spirituality-people-source.md.
Faithfulness
Repo data Entirely derived; no interpretive placement. The seed brief's "by tradition or quadrant" placement was rejected: assigning Habermas to a quadrant would be our claim, not Wilber's.
Effort
M (needs counts rechecked against the full text; current numbers are a first pass).
Risks
Low "click" for a newcomer; it rewards someone already reading the book. Mention counts over-weight polemic targets (Foucault) relative to quiet influences (Koestler).
10 · grand poster

A hundred models, one spine

The Integral Psychology correlation charts as a transit map: Wilber's basic structures run up the middle, and every theorist's stage sequence is a coloured line with its own station names alongside.

Shows
Wilber's central empirical claim in one image: that many independent stage models line up, and where each one stops.
Audience
Teachers and the already-curious; a signature poster for the site; header for charts/index.
Sources
All of charts/chart-1…11 (every alignment is a row in those tables); IP backmatter via library/2000-integral-psychology-full-text.md and the scanned images.
Faithfulness
Wilber's structure The alignments are his, cell by cell. Interpretation A transit map implies continuity between stations and equal spacing; his charts are tables with gaps and "approximate" alignments. Spacing must follow his rows, and gaps must be drawn as gaps.
Effort
L The chart pages are first-pass transcriptions, not yet re-verified; a poster would freeze any error in place.
Risks
Beautiful and risky: it makes the correlations look more exact than Wilber claims, and it is the one option a critic would most enjoy attacking. Needs the chart verification pass first.
11 · explainer diagram / poster

The Great Circle

Descent and Ascent on one circle, with Plotinus's arithmetic on the arcs, and the two pathologies as the places you fall off.

Shows
Involution/evolution, Eros/Agape and Ascent/Descent as one mechanism rather than three vocabulary pages; the Plotinian "minuses" and the two named failure modes.
Audience
Reader who has got through Book One of SES and hits the Plotinus chapters; header for concepts/involution-and-evolution, eros-and-agape, ascent-and-descent.
Sources
concepts/involution-and-evolution.md, concepts/eros-and-agape.md, concepts/ascent-and-descent.md; SES ch. 9 ("This integration may be thought of… as a Great Circle… 'The way up is the way down'"; the A, A+B / A, A−B footnote); SES ch. 12 ("Moving upward from the center (matter, the most fundamental) is… evolution (Reflux or Ascent, driven by Eros), and moving downward from spirit (the most significant) is involution"); Phobos/Thanatos in ch. 9.
Faithfulness
Wilber's structure The circle, both arcs, the arithmetic, the pole labels and both pathologies are his, with his own health warning about the picture. Interpretation Drawing Phobos/Thanatos as arrows leaving the circle is ours.
Effort
M
Risks
Dense for a newcomer; this is a second-read diagram. The "most significant / most fundamental" inversion needs the Kosmos-vs-cosmos page beside it or it reads as a value ranking.
12 · social share system (wilberwiki-4qc)

A card system for sharing

Not one image but a grammar: a glyph per core term, a one-line gloss, a verbatim line when there is one that fits, the site name. Generated per page, consistent everywhere.

Shows
A recognisable identity for the site in feeds; one card per concept, people and works page.
Audience
Someone sharing a link; the Open Graph preview is the first image most visitors see.
Sources
Frontmatter and first paragraph of each concepts/*.md, people/*.md, works/*.md; glyphs derived from options 1, 3, 4, 5, 11.
Faithfulness
Glosses are the wiki's own one-liners (as on concepts/index.md), labelled as such; any quoted line must pass verify_quotes.py.
Effort
S for the template; M to generate per page (a build step or a static batch of SVG → PNG, since Flowershow needs an image URL per page).
Risks
Needs a place to put per-page images and a way to set og:image per page in Flowershow (to check). Glyphs must stay abstract enough not to be read as the book figures.

Ranked recommendation

Ranked by "does it make one idea click for a newcomer, and would someone pin or share it", weighted by how little it has to invent. The first two are built; the gallery is the checkpoint, nothing here goes live without Rufus's pick.

  1. The pre/trans fallacy, in one picture (05). Wilber's structure throughout, a real "aha", works as a static poster and a share card, cheap. Built: pre-trans-fallacy/.
  2. Taking states off the ladder (07). The single most useful correction to how people read Wilber, and the one "what changed between books" visual that the repo can fully source. Built: states-and-stages/. It also doubles as a pilot for what the at6.4 track could become (below).
  3. One thought, four windows (01). Cheapest of all and the obvious homepage/OG image. Ranked below the two above only because every integral site already has a quadrant grid; the concrete-example treatment is what would make ours worth looking at.
  4. Pull a rung out (04). The best candidate for a third build: an interaction that performs Wilber's own thought experiment.
  5. Wilber I–V: the books on a line (08). Should exist on works/phases; sourced from the catalog; deliberately arrow-free.
  6. The Great Circle (11). Strong for second-read visitors; dense for first.
  7. The Big Three become the Big One (02). Good teaching slide; modest as an image.
  8. The Great Nest, with depth and span (03). The prettiest wall poster, but needs the most care to stay clear of the IP figure.
  9. Your own psychograph (06). Fun, but likely to be misread as a quiz; build only with firm framing.
  10. A card system for sharing (12). Do it once 1, 4 and 5 exist to supply glyphs; depends on a Flowershow og:image check.
  11. Who lives where in SES (09). Sound and cheap, low newcomer value; a nice-to-have on the people index.
  12. A hundred models, one spine (10). Park until the chart transcriptions are verified; too easy to make Wilber's claims look more exact than he makes them.

Where they would go

Concept-page header images for 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 11 (Flowershow renders raw HTML in markdown, and a page-scoped stylesheet can be added via config.json head exactly as assets/roadmap/roadmap.css is today). Option 7 could also sit on works/phases under IV → V and on concepts/from-ses-to-integral-spirituality. Option 8 on works/phases. Option 9 on people/index. Option 12 is site-wide once per-page og:image is confirmed possible.

On the concept-lineage track (wilberwiki-at6.4)

Change its shape rather than continue or park it. The graph-of-edges target is what makes the research chain so long: every edge is a historical priority claim that needs its own evidence, and the September review rightly refused to let recurrence or similarity count as inheritance. Option 7 suggests a cheaper target that keeps the same honesty: one before/after diagram per transition (I → II, II → III, III → IV, IV → V), each showing a single formulation as it was drawn in an earlier book and as it was drawn later, with the passages beside it. No edges, no derivation claims, just "here is what the picture looked like, then". The existing ledger stays useful as the vocabulary and evidence store; the prototype-evaluation and decision gates (.4.5, .4.6) still apply to each diptych. Full argument in RECOMMENDATION.md.
