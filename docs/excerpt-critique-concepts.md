# Excerpt critique: `concepts/*.md`

Critical editorial review of the excerpt choices on all 17 concept pages, done after the
accordion/foldable-callout formatting was removed and quotes became directly visible (no
"expand for more" fallback). Reviewed against the full text at
`library/1995-sex-ecology-spirituality-full-text.md`, reading a page or two of surrounding
context around every quote, not just the cited lines.

This is a critique only — no concept pages were edited as part of this pass.

**Recurring issue found across multiple pages, flagged once here rather than per-page:**
several pages mis-cite the chapter a quote comes from (attributing it to "Book 1," to a
plausible-sounding but wrong chapter title, or presenting an endnote as if it were body
text). This showed up on 6 of the 17 pages, independently, across all four review passes —
worth a dedicated citation-accuracy sweep across the whole concept wiki, separate from the
content questions below.

## Cross-page summary

**Citation-accuracy issue across the wiki:** 6 of 17 pages mis-cite a chapter, either by
labeling a quote "Book 1" instead of the actual chapter number, attributing it to a
plausible-sounding but wrong chapter, or presenting an endnote as body text without saying
so (`big-three`, `dominator-vs-growth-hierarchy`, `great-chain-of-being`, `holarchy`,
`kosmos-vs-cosmos`, `postmodernism`, `pre-trans-fallacy`, `involution-and-evolution` — several
pages have more than one instance). This is mechanical to fix and worth doing as a single
pass across all pages, using `wilber-on-plato.md`'s footnote-labeling as the model for how
to handle endnote-sourced quotes.

**Pages most needing a follow-up excerpt pass, in priority order:**

1. **`dominator-vs-growth-hierarchy.md`** — worst of the set: three mislabeled citations, one
   quote that ends mid-sentence with an apologetic editorial note flagging its own brokenness,
   and no quote backing the page's foundational claim that holarchy isn't inherently
   oppressive. Concrete fixes given above for all three.
2. **`great-chain-of-being.md`** — the page never actually defines the Great Chain in any
   quote, only narrates its decline; the missing definitional quote is a one-line fix with a
   strong candidate identified above.
3. **`kosmos-vs-cosmos.md`** — three citation errors (two ch.1→ch.2, one ch.12→ch.4); the
   quotes themselves need no content changes, just correct labels.
4. **`postmodernism.md`** — one real chapter mislabel (ch.14 claimed, actually ch.2 endnotes)
   plus a clearly sharper unused passage (the "Pluralistic relativism was the only acceptable
   worldview..." self-refutation quote) that would strengthen the page's central argument.
5. **`right-hand-and-left-hand-paths.md`** — the synthesis explicitly describes Wilber's own
   worked example (the thought/brain-physiology correlation) as evidence, but no quote on the
   page actually gives that example; a direct replacement is identified above.

**Pages that are already strong and need no substantive work:** `ascent-and-descent`,
`eros-and-agape` (aside from one optional swap), `four-quadrants`, `holons`, `systems-theory`,
`vision-logic`, `wilber-on-plato`.
