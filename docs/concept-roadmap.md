# Concept Roadmap — design doc

The source of truth for [`roadmap.md`](../roadmap.md): **what order should someone meet
Wilber's ideas in, which are optional detours, and how does a non-expert get in at all?**
Written as structure first, so the visual is generated from it rather than invented in
HTML — when this doc changes, the page changes.

Inspiration: [roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh) — a central vertical **spine** you follow
top to bottom, with **side branches** for depth, counterargument, and applications.

## Who this is for

**A non-expert who has heard of Wilber and bounced off him.** That reader is the whole
design constraint. They do not arrive wanting to learn about holons; they arrive with a
felt problem — science and spirituality both seem half-right and won't sit together; the
culture war seems stupid but they can't say why; therapy and meditation seem to be doing
different jobs. The roadmap has to open on *their* question, not Wilber's vocabulary.

Two consequences that drove the revision:

- **The door is the Great Chain of Being, not Kosmos vs. Cosmos.** An earlier draft
  opened on the Kosmos/cosmos spelling distinction, which invites the honest reaction
  *"I don't care how you spell it."* The Great Chain is older, warmer, and instantly
  graspable — *here is the picture nearly every premodern culture had; Wilber's whole
  project is asking whether they were onto something.* Kosmos vs. Cosmos then lands as
  stop 2: what modernity dropped.
- **Nineteen stops is a wall, so there are two routes on one diagram.** Six stops are
  marked as **the essential six** — a genuinely coherent minimal Wilber (the units, the
  map, the ladder, the diagnosis, the guardrail, the payoff). Everything else is
  enrichment. A reader can see a two-hour path and a two-week path at once.

## Design principles

1. **The spine is a single, defensible reading order.** Each stop is a prerequisite for
   the next. If you can skip it without the next stop becoming unintelligible, it's a
   branch.
2. **Branches are optional on a first pass.** Depth, counterargument, historical detail,
   applications. Spine-only should still leave you with a coherent Wilber.
3. **Every concept page appears exactly once.** No orphans, no duplicates. Cross-links
   between pages already do the "see also" job; the roadmap's only job is *sequence*, and
   duplication ruins sequence.
4. **Order by dependency, not by Wilber's book order.** *SES* builds the apparatus in
   Book One and the history in Book Two — a good order for an argument, a punishing one
   for a newcomer.
5. **Plain-English act titles.** Each act names a claim, not a category. "The Vertical"
   became "Growing up doesn't stop where you think."
6. **Give skeptics somewhere to stand.** Every concept page already carries an honest
   "what's contestable" passage; the roadmap surfaces them rather than hiding them.

## Framing the page must carry

**This is Wilber circa 1995.** Every page is sourced from *Sex, Ecology, Spirituality* —
the phase-4 turn in a body of work with five acknowledged phases, and he revised real
things afterward. Presenting these ideas as timeless is the most common failure of
popular Wilber treatments. The page carries a banner saying so, linking to
[`bio.md`](../bio.md) and [`works/`](../works/index.md).

**Two entrances, not one.** Newcomers start at stop 1. But most real arrivals are
topical — people search *Wilber on postmodernism*, *Wilber environmental ethics*, *Wilber
on gender*, not *holarchy*. The "Wilber on X" applications are therefore a labelled side
entrance at the top of the diagram, not a footnote at the bottom, with a route back onto
the spine.

## The spine

Four acts, nineteen spine stops. ★ marks the essential six.

### ACT I — The universe has an inside

*The ontology: what sort of thing exists, and what's the basic unit?*
Anchor line: *"the Great Chain of Being... the single most persistent worldview in
history."*

**1. The Great Chain of Being** — `great-chain-of-being`
The door. Matter → life → mind → spirit: the picture nearly every premodern culture
converged on. Wilber's project is to ask whether it survives modern evidence.
→ *no branches — this is the door*

**2. Kosmos vs. Cosmos** — `kosmos-vs-cosmos`
What modernity dropped. Is reality the physical universe, or matter *plus* life, mind,
and spirit? Everything else follows from which one you think you live in.

**★ 3. Holons** — `holons`
The basic unit: everything is a whole that is also a part. Not things, not processes —
whole/parts, all the way up and down.
- ↳ `systems-theory` — the closest respectable rival, and where Wilber says it falls short
- ↳ `two-arrows-of-time` — the puzzle holons are partly meant to solve: physics says
  decay, biology says ascent
- ↳ `eros-and-agape` — **first sense**: the vertical and horizontal drives of *every*
  holon (with Phobos and Thanatos as their pathologies). Forward-references stop 16,
  where the same pair becomes the motive force of Ascent and Descent.

**4. Holarchy** — `holarchy`
How holons nest — and the word that costs Wilber more readers than any other:
*hierarchy*.
- ↳ `dominator-vs-growth-hierarchy` — the defence, and a distinction the rest of the
  book depends on
- ↳ `great-plenitude` — Lovejoy's principle read as a research programme

**5. Interiority** — `interiority`
Every holon has an inside as well as an outside. The single move separating Wilber from
every systems theorist he otherwise resembles.
- ↳ `limits-of-the-exterior-approach` — why no amount of empirical science reaches the
  inside

### ACT II — Four faces of every moment

*Interiority plus holarchy gives you a grid. The grid is the famous part — this is what
people mean by AQAL.*
Anchor line: *"every holon has these four facets... and each requires its own mode of
knowing."*

**★ 6. The Four Quadrants** — `four-quadrants`
Interior/exterior × individual/collective. AQAL's load-bearing diagram.
- ↳ `micro-macro-coevolution` — why the left and right columns can never be collapsed
  into one another

**7. The Big Three** — `big-three`
I / We / It — art, morals, science. The quadrants in their classical, pre-Wilber
clothing (Kant's three critiques, Popper's three worlds).
- ↳ `right-hand-and-left-hand-paths` — the two fundamentally different kinds of language
  the quadrants call for

**★ 8. Flatland** — `flatland`
The diagnosis, and only nameable now that you have the grid: modernity collapsed the
Left-Hand quadrants into the Right, re-describing everything interior as an "it."
- ↳ `dignity-and-disaster-of-modernity` — the fair version: differentiating the value
  spheres was a real achievement *before* it became a dissociation

### ACT III — Growing up doesn't stop where you think

*Holarchy applied to consciousness. This is where Wilber gets contentious.*
Anchor line: *"you have to be somebody before you can be nobody."*

**★ 9. Waves and Streams** — `waves-and-streams`
Levels of consciousness vs. the developmental lines running through them — why someone
can be cognitively advanced and morally stunted.

**10. The Dialectic of Progress** — `dialectic-of-progress`
Every stage solves the last one's central problem and creates a new one. The structural
rebuttal to both "it all keeps getting better" and "we've fallen from Eden."
- ↳ `primal-ecological-wisdom` — the worked example: why "tribal societies had ecological
  wisdom" confuses indissociation with integration

**★ 11. The Pre/Trans Fallacy** — `pre-trans-fallacy`
**The guardrail.** Prerational and transrational both look nonrational, so they get
confused — reduced downward (Freud) or elevated upward (Jung, Campbell). Almost every
misreading of Wilber, in either direction, is this error made before the reader had the
concept to name it. Nothing after this point is readable without it.
→ *no branches — read it twice instead*

**12. Vision-Logic** — `vision-logic`
The first stop past formal rationality: postformal, network-and-context cognition —
and where Wilber locates *us*, now.
- ↳ `postmodernism` — Wilber's actual claim is that postmodernism **is** early
  vision-logic: its genuine achievement (aperspectival pluralism) and its derailment
  (aperspectival madness). *(An earlier draft filed this under stop 18, which
  misrepresented the argument.)*

**13. The Transpersonal Domains** — `transpersonal-domains`
Development continuing past the personal. "Personal plus, not personal minus" — exactly
the claim stop 11 was protecting.
- ↳ `psychic-subtle-causal-nondual` — the four stages, named and distinguished

**14. Validity Claims of Mysticism** — `validity-claims-of-mysticism`
The epistemology stop, and Wilber's most interesting move: contemplative claims can be
checked, if you're willing to run the experiment.
- ↳ `reconstruction-of-the-contemplative-path` — injunction / apprehension / confirmation,
  spelled out

### ACT IV — How the West lost its inside

*Two thousand years of getting it half-right — and what Spirit has to do with any of it.*
Anchor line: *"every neurosis is a miniature ecological crisis."*

**★ 15. Ascent and Descent** — `ascent-and-descent`
The two eternal movements: wisdom's return to the One, compassion's embrace of the Many.
*SES* Book Two is the history of their divorce.
- ↳ `wilber-on-plato` — where both are still held together, and the misreading that split
  them
- ↳ `the-two-gods` — the transcendent God vs. the immanent "schizoid God"

**16. Eros and Agape** — `eros-and-agape`
**Second sense** (see stop 3): the same two drives, now as the motive forces behind
Ascent and Descent — and their pathologies, Phobos and Thanatos.
- ↳ `repression` — the pathology at civilizational scale

**17. The Ego Camp and the Eco Camp** — `ego-camp-and-eco-camp`
*SES*'s central polemic, and the reason the book runs 800 pages: Ascenders and
Descenders, each half-right, each unable to hear the other.
- ↳ `agony-of-modernity-fichte-vs-spinoza` — the split at its 19th-century sharpest
- ↳ `legacy-of-the-idealists` — the one real attempt at synthesis, and why it collapsed

**18. The Death of God** — `death-of-god`
Not secularization as loss of faith, but as the Descended camp's total historical
victory — flatland becoming common sense.

**19. Involution and Evolution** — `involution-and-evolution`
The final turn: evolution as Spirit's unfolding *back* toward itself, having first
enfolded itself into matter.
- ↳ `spirit-in-action` — the panentheism the whole system was built to make sayable

## Side entrance: Wilber on X

Applications, not prerequisites — and the way most real readers arrive. Placed
at the **top** of the diagram as a labelled alternate entrance, with a route back to the
spine.

- `postmodernism` — *(also branch of stop 12; the one deliberate exception to the
  appears-once rule, because it is both the commonest search term and a genuine part of
  the vision-logic argument. It is rendered as an entrance link, not a second node.)*
- `environmental-ethics` — Ground-value, intrinsic value, instrumental value
- `sex-and-gender` — his account of sex differences in development
- `sexuality-and-modernity` — sex under modernity's dissociation
- `multiculturalism-and-transnationalism` — both read as stage phenomena

## Side rail: where Wilber is most contested

A persistent rail linking the honest "what's contestable" passages already written into
the pages, so skeptical readers have somewhere to stand rather than bouncing. Current
picks: `pre-trans-fallacy`, `dominator-vs-growth-hierarchy`, `environmental-ethics`,
`primal-ecological-wisdom`, `sex-and-gender`, `dialectic-of-progress`.

## Coverage check

41 concept pages, each placed once (plus `postmodernism` also surfaced as an entrance
link): 19 spine stops + 18 branches + 4 side-entrance applications.
**Any new concept page must be placed on the spine or a branch as part of adding it, or
the roadmap silently rots.**

## Known gaps this exposed

Concept pages the wiki is missing, surfaced by trying to sequence what exists:

- **"Transcend and include"** — Wilber's single most-quoted phrase, currently only
  mentioned in passing on `eros-and-agape` and `repression`.
- **AQAL as a named framework** — the term the outside world knows him by, currently
  only inside `four-quadrants`.
- **Depth vs. span** — buried inside `holarchy`.
- **The twenty tenets** as a set — referenced on twelve pages, defined on none.

Tracked as a follow-up batch, deliberately not blocking the roadmap.

## Not in scope (yet)

A **second, separate roadmap for the works** — a reading path through Wilber's books
(which to start with, which are the phase-shifts, which are optional), keyed to
`works/`. Different question, different diagram; deliberately not mixed in.

## Visual/technical notes

- Page is `roadmap.md` at the repo root, rendered at `/roadmap`.
- Frontmatter `layout: plain`, `showSidebar: false`, `showToc: false` — confirmed against
  Flowershow's docs, not guessed.
- Built as MDX/JSX blocks with Tailwind classes (`className`, not `class`) — the
  documented Flowershow path. No `<style>` tags, no SVG, no arbitrary-value Tailwind
  classes: connectors are borders on divs, so nothing depends on undocumented behaviour.
- Central vertical spine, branches alternating left/right, acts separated by a coloured
  band. Essential-six nodes carry a ★ and a heavier ring.
- Dark/light aware via Tailwind `dark:` variants.
