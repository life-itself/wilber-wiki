# Concept Roadmap — design doc

The source of truth for the roadmap page: **what order should someone meet Wilber's
ideas in, and which ones are optional detours?** Written first as structure, so the
visual can be built from it (and rebuilt when it changes) rather than invented in HTML.

Inspiration: [roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh) — a central vertical **spine** you follow
top to bottom, with **side branches** hanging off each stop for depth, alternatives, and
"this is where the arguments happen." The spine is a claim about pedagogy, not about
Wilber's own chapter order.

## Design principles

1. **The spine is a single, defensible reading order.** Each stop is genuinely a
   prerequisite for the next. If you can skip it without the next stop becoming
   unintelligible, it belongs on a branch.
2. **Branches are optional on a first pass.** They're depth, counterargument, historical
   detail, or applications. Someone who reads only the spine should come away with a
   coherent Wilber.
3. **Every concept page appears exactly once.** No page is orphaned; no page is on both
   a spine and a branch. (Cross-links between pages already do the "see also" job — the
   roadmap's job is *sequence*, and sequence is ruined by duplication.)
4. **Order by dependency, not by Wilber's own book order.** *SES* builds the systematic
   apparatus in Book One and the history in Book Two; that's a good order for an
   argument and a punishing one for a newcomer. Notably, Flatland is *SES*'s
   destination but is unintelligible before the quadrants — so here it comes right
   after them.

## Why not Wilber's own order

*SES* opens with twenty tenets of holons and closes 800 pages later with the Kosmos
being unpacked. The roadmap keeps the same skeleton but front-loads the **motivating
question** (what kind of universe is this?) and moves the **guardrail** (the pre/trans
fallacy) earlier than Wilber does, because almost every misreading of Wilber — in both
directions, dismissing him as a mystic or embracing him as one — is a pre/trans error
made before the reader had the concept to name it.

## The spine

Four acts, eighteen spine stops. Each stop lists its branches.

### ACT I — The universe has an inside

*The ontology. What sort of thing exists, and what's the basic unit?*

**1. Kosmos vs. Cosmos** — `kosmos-vs-cosmos`
The opening question, and the whole book in one distinction: is reality the physical
universe (cosmos), or matter *plus* life, mind, and spirit (Kosmos)? Everything else
follows from which one you think you live in.
→ *no branches — this is the door*

**2. Holons** — `holons`
The basic unit: everything is a whole that is also a part. Not things, not processes —
whole/parts, all the way up and down.
- ↳ `systems-theory` — the closest respectable rival account, and where Wilber says it
  falls short
- ↳ `two-arrows-of-time` — the puzzle holons are partly meant to solve: why physics
  says decay and biology says ascent

**3. Holarchy** — `holarchy`
How holons nest. Introduces the word that costs Wilber more readers than any other:
*hierarchy*.
- ↳ `dominator-vs-growth-hierarchy` — the defence, and the distinction the whole book
  needs you to accept

**4. Interiority** — `interiority`
Every holon has an inside as well as an outside. The single move that separates Wilber
from every systems theorist he otherwise resembles.
- ↳ `limits-of-the-exterior-approach` — why no amount of empirical science reaches the
  inside

### ACT II — The map

*Interiority plus holarchy gives you a grid. The grid is the famous part.*

**5. The Four Quadrants** — `four-quadrants`
Interior/exterior × individual/collective. AQAL's load-bearing diagram.
- ↳ `micro-macro-coevolution` — why the left and right columns can never be collapsed
  into one another

**6. The Big Three** — `big-three`
I / We / It — art, morals, science; the quadrants in their classical, pre-Wilber
clothing (Kant's three critiques, Popper's three worlds).
- ↳ `right-hand-and-left-hand-paths` — the two fundamentally different kinds of
  language the quadrants call for

**7. Flatland** — `flatland`
The diagnosis. Modernity collapsed the Left-Hand quadrants into the Right — everything
interior re-described as an "it." Now nameable, because you have the grid.
- ↳ `dignity-and-disaster-of-modernity` — the fair version: modernity's differentiation
  of the value spheres was a genuine achievement *before* it became a dissociation

### ACT III — The vertical

*Holarchy applied to consciousness. This is where Wilber gets contentious.*

**8. Waves and Streams** — `waves-and-streams`
Levels of consciousness vs. the developmental lines that move through them — why
someone can be cognitively advanced and morally stunted.
- ↳ `great-chain-of-being` — the traditional matter/life/mind/spirit ladder Wilber is
  reconstructing
- ↳ `great-plenitude` — Lovejoy's principle, read as a research programme

**9. The Dialectic of Progress** — `dialectic-of-progress`
Every stage solves the last stage's central problem and creates a new one. The
structural rebuttal to both "it all keeps getting better" and "we've fallen from Eden."
- ↳ `primal-ecological-wisdom` — the worked example: why "tribal societies had
  ecological wisdom" confuses indissociation with integration

**10. The Pre/Trans Fallacy** — `pre-trans-fallacy`
**The guardrail.** Prerational and transrational both look nonrational, so they get
confused — reduced downward (Freud) or elevated upward (Jung, Campbell). Nothing after
this point is readable without it.
→ *no branches — read it twice instead*

**11. Vision-Logic** — `vision-logic`
The first stop past formal rationality: the postformal, network-and-context cognition
Wilber thinks the present moment is straining toward.

**12. The Transpersonal Domains** — `transpersonal-domains`
Development continuing past the personal. "Personal plus, not personal minus" — which
is exactly the claim stop 10 was protecting.
- ↳ `psychic-subtle-causal-nondual` — the four stages, named and distinguished

**13. Validity Claims of Mysticism** — `validity-claims-of-mysticism`
The epistemology stop, and Wilber's most interesting move: contemplative claims can be
checked, if you're willing to run the experiment.
- ↳ `reconstruction-of-the-contemplative-path` — the injunction/apprehension/
  confirmation method spelled out

### ACT IV — The story and the stakes

*Two thousand years of getting it half-right, and what Spirit has to do with any of it.*

**14. Ascent and Descent** — `ascent-and-descent`
The two eternal movements: wisdom's return to the One, compassion's embrace of the
Many. *SES* Book Two is the history of their divorce.
- ↳ `wilber-on-plato` — where both are still held together, and the misreading that
  split them
- ↳ `the-two-gods` — the transcendent God vs. the immanent "schizoid God"

**15. Eros and Agape** — `eros-and-agape`
The motive forces behind the two movements — and, crucially, their pathologies
(Phobos and Thanatos).
- ↳ `repression` — the pathology at civilizational scale: "every neurosis is a
  miniature ecological crisis"

**16. The Ego Camp and the Eco Camp** — `ego-camp-and-eco-camp`
*SES*'s central polemic, and the reason the book is 800 pages: Ascenders and Descenders,
each half-right, each unable to hear the other.
- ↳ `agony-of-modernity-fichte-vs-spinoza` — the split at its 19th-century sharpest
- ↳ `legacy-of-the-idealists` — the one attempt at synthesis, and why it collapsed

**17. The Death of God** — `death-of-god`
Not secularization as loss of faith, but as the Descended camp's total historical
victory — flatland becoming common sense.
- ↳ `postmodernism` — the sequel: its real achievement and its derailment

**18. Involution and Evolution** — `involution-and-evolution`
The final turn: evolution as Spirit's unfolding *back* toward itself, having first
enfolded itself into matter.
- ↳ `spirit-in-action` — the panentheism the whole system was built to make sayable

## Off-spine: Wilber on X

Applications, not prerequisites. Reachable at any point after Act III; presented as a
cluster beside the spine's tail rather than as branches of a single stop, because each
one draws on several stops at once.

- `environmental-ethics` — Ground-value, intrinsic value, instrumental value
- `sex-and-gender` — his account of sex differences in development
- `sexuality-and-modernity` — sex under modernity's dissociation
- `multiculturalism-and-transnationalism` — both read as stage phenomena

## Coverage check

41 concept pages, each appearing exactly once: 18 spine stops + 19 branches + 4
off-spine applications. Any new concept page must be placed on the spine or a branch as
part of adding it, or the roadmap silently rots.

## Visual notes

- Central vertical spine, top to bottom, numbered stops. Branches alternate left/right.
- Acts get a heading band across the spine so the four-movement structure is visible at
  a glance.
- Spine nodes and branch nodes should be visually distinct enough that "what's optional"
  reads instantly — roadmap.sh does this with fill colour and border weight.
- Full-bleed page: no wiki sidebars, own layout, dark/light aware.
