# Chart 9: Sociocultural Evolution

Correlates a dozen theorists' accounts of social/cultural evolution against the
familiar foraging → horticultural → agrarian → industrial → informational sequence
Wilber uses throughout the book, rather than against the full individual
basic-structures spine (which is shown in the source but mostly sparse/bracket-only at
this resolution — presented here at the sequence's own natural grouping instead of
forcing false precision onto the finer spine).

Transcribed and cross-checked against the source page images
(`library/2000-integral-psychology-images/00050.jpg`-`00053.jpg`); draft, not yet
independently verified by a second pass.
