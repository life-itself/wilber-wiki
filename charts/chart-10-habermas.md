# Chart 10: Jürgen Habermas

Habermas's own developmental scheme, correlated against Wilber's basic-structures
spine on one page, and presented at Habermas's own seven-stage resolution (idea of the
good life / domain of validity / ethics) on the other — a different, more specific
scheme than the coarser precon/con/postcon/post-postcon mapping used elsewhere.

Transcribed and cross-checked against the source page images
(`library/2000-integral-psychology-images/00054.jpg`-`00055.jpg`); draft, not yet
independently verified by a second pass. Table 1's source data is sparse at this image
resolution — presented at a coarser grouping than [Chart 1](chart-1-wilber-correlations.md)
rather than guessing fine row boundaries.
