# Chart 11: James Mark Baldwin

Baldwin's own five-level scheme (prelogical, quasilogical, logical, extralogical,
hyperlogical — see [his people page](../people/baldwin.md)) correlated against his
own further breakdowns: logical mode, the "psychic objects" characteristic of each
level, the dualism resolved or introduced at each level, and stages of aesthetic,
religious, and ethical development.

Transcribed and cross-checked against the source page images
(`library/2000-integral-psychology-images/00056.jpg`-`00057.jpg`); draft, not yet
independently verified by a second pass. Presented at Baldwin's own five-level
resolution — the source data doesn't cleanly subdivide further than that, unlike
[Chart 1](chart-1-wilber-correlations.md)'s finer basic-structures spine.
