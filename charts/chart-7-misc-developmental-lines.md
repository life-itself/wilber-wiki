# Chart 7: Miscellaneous Developmental Lines

A shorter chart correlating several less-central developmental models against the
basic-structures spine, plus a second table (a different row spine — see
[Chart 1](chart-1-wilber-correlations.md) for why these don't force-merge) correlating
modal-experience, empathy, and gender/affect models.

Transcribed and cross-checked against the source page images
(`library/2000-integral-psychology-images/00046.jpg`-`00047.jpg`); draft, not yet
independently verified by a second pass. Some cells in the source are sparse or
ambiguously positioned at this image resolution — marked "—" rather than guessed.
