# Chart 8: Miscellaneous

Two more short correlation tables: Howard Gardner's four-wave model of development
overlaid on the familiar basic-structures spine, and a separate table correlating
stages of art (Wilber's own scheme) with Melvin Miller's teleological/ateleological/
antiteleological worldview types.

Transcribed and cross-checked against the source page images
(`library/2000-integral-psychology-images/00048.jpg`-`00049.jpg`); draft, not yet
independently verified by a second pass.

## Universal waves of development (Gardner)

Gardner's own model only subdivides into four broad waves in the source, not
row-for-row against the full basic-structures spine — presented here at Gardner's own
resolution rather than forcing a false precision. The rough correspondence to the
basic-structures spine (following the general precon/con/postcon/post-postcon mapping
used throughout the book — see
[Chart 1](chart-1-wilber-correlations.md#fulcrums-pathology-treatment-and-moral-span)'s
fulcrum table) is: preconventional ≈ sensorimotor through rep-mind, conventional ≈
conop, postconventional ≈ formop/postformal, post-postconventional ≈ the transpersonal
waves.
