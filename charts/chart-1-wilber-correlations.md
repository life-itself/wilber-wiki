# Chart 1: Wilber Correlations

The book's own master correlation chart, redrawn here as text instead of a scanned
page image. It lines up Wilber's basic structures/levels (the "Great Nest" spine used
throughout his work) against several parallel readings of the same territory: the
self-sense and its defenses at each level, the felt affect and the corresponding scope
of moral consideration, the fulcrum/pathology/treatment picture used in his
psychotherapy model, and — a genuinely separate developmental line — gender identity
and the worldview that goes with it.

Transcribed and cross-checked against the source page images
(`library/2000-integral-psychology-images/00016.jpg`–`00019.jpg`); not yet independently
verified by a second pass — treat cell contents as draft until reviewed. Rendering
confirmed working (checked the published page's raw HTML: all rowSpan grouping and
cell content intact).
