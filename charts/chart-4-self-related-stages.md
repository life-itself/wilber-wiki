# Chart 4: Self-Related Stages

The largest of the appendix charts: fifteen researchers of self/ego development,
spread across three sub-parts (4A, 4B, 4C in the source) and three different row
spines — the familiar basic-structures spine (Loevinger, Broughton, Neumann,
Cook-Greuter), Wilber's own numbered fulcrums F-1 through F-9 (Sullivan/Grant/Grant,
Wade, Washburn, Erikson, Graves, Kegan — merged into one table below since both source
pages use the identical fulcrum spine), and a third, coarser spine of Scheler's five
"structural hardware" levels (Pascual-Leone, Jaspers, Steiner, Beck).

Transcribed and cross-checked against the source page images
(`library/2000-integral-psychology-images/00028.jpg`-`00033.jpg`); draft, not yet
independently verified by a second pass.
