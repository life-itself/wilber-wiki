# Chart 2: Basic Structures in Other Systems

Correlates the basic-structures spine against eleven other systems: Huston Smith,
Plotinus, the Buddhist vijnanas, Aurobindo, Kabbalah, and the General Great Chain on
the familiar individual-development spine; Stan Grof, John Battista, the chakras, and
James Mark Baldwin on Grof's own perinatal-matrix spine; and Vedanta's own
sheaths/states scheme correlated with William Tiller, Leadbetter (Theosophy), and
Adi Da.

Transcribed and cross-checked against the source page images
(`library/2000-integral-psychology-images/00020.jpg`-`00023.jpg`); draft, not yet
independently verified by a second pass.
