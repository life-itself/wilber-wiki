# Chart 6: Stages of Spirituality

The contemplative-traditions chart referenced directly in the body text (see
[Is Spirituality Developmental? The Five Definitions](../concepts/five-definitions-of-spirituality.md)) —
Sufism, Buddhist Mahamudra, Christian and Islamic mystics, Patanjali's Yoga Sutras, and
a dozen Western researchers of faith/spiritual development, across three sub-parts (6A,
6B, 6C in the source).

Transcribed and cross-checked against the source page images
(`library/2000-integral-psychology-images/00040.jpg`-`00045.jpg`); draft, not yet
independently verified by a second pass.

## Highest Yoga Tantra

A self-contained esoteric sequence discussed directly in the body text (see the quoted
passage on [Is Spirituality Developmental?](../concepts/five-definitions-of-spirituality.md))
— presented here as its own linear progression rather than forced onto the
basic-structures spine. The source also names three "post-Enlightenment" states —
savikalpa (luminosity, deity form), nirvikalpa (cessation), and sahaja ("non-meditation",
one taste) — running alongside this sequence rather than as further steps within it.
