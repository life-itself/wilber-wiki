# Chart 5: The Self-Related Stages of Morals and Perspectives

Fourteen researchers of moral development and perspective-taking, across three
sub-parts (5A, 5B, 5C in the source). Two of the source pages (037 and 039) turn out to
repeat data already reconstructed on [Chart 1](chart-1-wilber-correlations.md) — the
worldviews table and the moral-span/fulcrum table respectively — so this page adds only
the new columns those pages introduce (Peck; Selman and Gilligan) rather than
re-transcribing what's already there.

Transcribed and cross-checked against the source page images
(`library/2000-integral-psychology-images/00034.jpg`-`00039.jpg`); draft, not yet
independently verified by a second pass.
