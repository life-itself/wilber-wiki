# Works by Ken Wilber

Working draft of the full bibliography, organized by year of first publication. Each entry
is a stub for the eventual works-page layout: a cover thumbnail, title/subtitle, year, format,
and a one- or two-sentence description. **Cover images are not yet sourced** — see the `cover:`
field left blank on every entry; fetching real cover art (Open Library / Google Books API by
ISBN, or scans from the publisher) is a follow-up task, see `docs/plan.md`.

Descriptions marked `[DRAFT]` are lower-confidence and should be checked before publishing;
most other descriptions are core, well-established characterizations of each book.

Format legend: **B** = book, **E** = editor/anthology, **CO** = co-authored, **eB** = ebook-only,
**A** = audio program.

---

## 1970s

### The Spectrum of Consciousness (1977) — B
- cover:
- Wilber's first book, written at 23. Maps Western psychology (Freud through humanistic
  psychology) and Eastern contemplative traditions onto a single "spectrum" of identity
  running from narrow ego to the whole cosmos. Established transpersonal psychology as a
  field and made Wilber's name.

### No Boundary: Eastern and Western Approaches to Personal Growth (1979) — B
- cover:
- A short, accessible companion to *Spectrum*, aimed at a general audience: growth as a
  progressive dissolving of felt boundaries between self and world, surveyed across
  therapeutic and contemplative approaches.

## 1980s

### The Atman Project: A Transpersonal View of Human Development (1980) — B
- cover:
- Extends the spectrum model into a full developmental map from infancy to enlightenment,
  arguing that human growth is a spiral return to (not regression toward) an original
  undifferentiated ground — the "Atman project." Introduces the **pre/trans fallacy**.

### Up from Eden: A Transpersonal View of Human Evolution (1981) — B
- cover:
- Applies the same developmental logic historically/anthropologically, tracing the
  evolution of human consciousness from archaic and magical structures through mythic,
  mental, and (prospectively) transpersonal stages of culture.

### The Holographic Paradigm and Other Paradoxes (1982) — E
- cover:
- Edited anthology exploring the "holographic paradigm" in physics and consciousness
  studies (Bohm, Pribram, and others), with a critical introduction and afterword by
  Wilber. `[DRAFT]`

### A Sociable God: A Brief Introduction to a Transcendental Sociology (1983) — B
- cover:
- A short, dense outline of a sociology of religion within Wilber's developmental
  framework — distinguishing "legitimate" religion (which stabilizes a given stage) from
  "authentic" religion (which transforms to a higher one).

### Eye to Eye: The Quest for the New Paradigm (1984) — B
- cover:
- Essay collection on Wilber's "three eyes of knowing" (sense, reason, contemplation) as
  distinct, valid modes of knowledge, and a critique of category errors made when one eye
  is used to judge data proper to another.

### Quantum Questions: Mystical Writings of the World's Great Physicists (1984) — E
- cover:
- Anthology of the mystical/philosophical writings of major 20th-century physicists
  (Einstein, Heisenberg, Schrödinger, and others), edited with a long introductory essay
  arguing physics does not, in itself, support mysticism the way popular "quantum
  mysticism" claims.

### Transformations of Consciousness: Conventional and Contemplative Perspectives on Development (1986) — CO
- cover:
- With Jack Engler and Daniel P. Brown. Academic, clinically oriented synthesis of
  Western developmental psychology and Buddhist contemplative maps of the path. `[DRAFT]`

### Spiritual Choices: The Problem of Recognizing Authentic Paths to Inner Transformation (1987) — CO
- cover:
- With Dick Anthony and Bruce Ecker. Proposes criteria for distinguishing genuine
  transformative spiritual paths from spiritually or psychologically harmful ones.
  `[DRAFT]`

## 1990s

### Grace and Grit: Spirituality and Healing in the Life and Death of Treya Killam Wilber (1991) — B
- cover:
- Memoir of Wilber's marriage to Treya Killam Wilber, her five-year illness with breast
  cancer, and her death, interleaving her journals with his own reflections. Wilber's
  most personal and, for many readers, most affecting book.

### Sex, Ecology, Spirituality: The Spirit of Evolution (1995, rev. 2001) — B
- cover:
- **SES.** Volume one of the projected *Kosmos Trilogy* and Wilber's central theoretical
  work: the full statement of the four-quadrant **AQAL** model, holons and holarchies, and
  an argument that evolution — cosmic, biological, and cultural — has a directionality
  toward greater depth and consciousness. Dense and academic; the book this wiki's
  annotation work is meant to start with.

### A Brief History of Everything (1996) — B
- cover:
- Accessible, interview-format restatement of *SES* for a general audience — the usual
  recommended entry point into Wilber's mature system.

### The Eye of Spirit: An Integral Vision for a World Gone Slightly Mad (1997) — B
- cover:
- Companion volume to *SES* applying the AQAL model to art, literary theory, ecology, and
  the science-and-religion debate; part two of the *Kosmos Trilogy* project (though not
  numbered as such on the cover).

### The Essential Ken Wilber: An Introductory Reader (1998) — E
- cover:
- Anthology of excerpts spanning his career to date, assembled as an introduction to
  newcomers.

### The Marriage of Sense and Soul: Integrating Science and Religion (1998) — B
- cover:
- Short trade book proposing a reconciliation of science and religion through Wilber's
  "three eyes of knowing," aimed at a general readership beyond his usual audience.

### One Taste: The Journals of Ken Wilber (1999, rev. 2000) — B
- cover:
- A year of Wilber's personal journals — daily reflection, meditation notes, and
  commentary on contemporaries — giving a more informal, first-person view of his life
  and practice than his theoretical books.

## 2000s

### Integral Psychology: Consciousness, Spirit, Psychology, Therapy (2000) — B
- cover:
- Systematic attempt to cross-index dozens of Western developmental-psychology models
  (Piaget, Kohlberg, Loevinger, Graves, and others) against contemplative stage models
  within the AQAL framework; includes Wilber's widely reproduced comparative charts.

### A Theory of Everything: An Integral Vision for Business, Politics, Science and Spirituality (2000) — B
- cover:
- Short, applied restatement of AQAL aimed explicitly at readers in business, politics,
  medicine, and education rather than psychology or spirituality specifically.

### Speaking of Everything (2001) — A
- cover:
- Audio program (interview/lecture format) covering the same ground as *A Theory of
  Everything*. `[DRAFT]`

### Boomeritis: A Novel That Will Set You Free (2002) — B
- cover:
- Wilber's one novel: a satire of postmodern academic and New Age culture (his term
  "Boomeritis" names a narcissistic strain of Baby Boomer postmodernism), following a
  young protagonist through a series of encounters that also function as an extended
  self-commentary and appendix on integral theory.

### Kosmic Consciousness (2003) — A
- cover:
- Long-form audio conversation series, wide-ranging interview covering the arc of
  Wilber's work up to that point. `[DRAFT]`

### The Simple Feeling of Being: Visionary, Spiritual, and Poetic Writings (2004) — E
- cover:
- Anthology of Wilber's more lyrical/visionary passages drawn from across his books,
  curated as a standalone contemplative reader.

### The Integral Operating System (2005) — B
- cover:
- Short practical/workbook introduction to AQAL as a diagnostic "operating system" for
  personal and organizational development, with accompanying exercises. `[DRAFT]`

### Integral Spirituality: A Startling New Role for Religion in the Modern and Postmodern World (2006) — B
- cover:
- Marks Wilber's turn to **"integral post-metaphysics"** — reinterpreting contemplative
  states and stages without pre-modern metaphysical assumptions — and introduces the
  **Wilber–Combs Lattice** mapping states of consciousness against structures/stages of
  development.

### The One Two Three of God (2006) — A
- cover:
- Audio program on first-, second-, and third-person approaches to the divine (I/We/It).
  `[DRAFT]`

### Integral Life Practice Starter Kit (2006) — CO
- cover:
- Multimedia practice kit (book, audio, workbook) introducing cross-training across
  body, mind, spirit, and shadow modules — precursor to the 2008 *Integral Life Practice*
  book. `[DRAFT]`

### The Integral Vision: A Very Short Introduction to the Revolutionary Integral Approach to Life, God, the Universe, and Everything (2007) — B
- cover:
- Wilber's shortest, most compressed general-audience introduction to AQAL — often
  recommended as a faster alternative to *A Brief History of Everything*.

### Integral Life Practice: A 21st-Century Blueprint for Physical Health, Emotional Balance, Mental Clarity, and Spiritual Awakening (2008) — CO
- cover:
- With Terry Patten, Adam Leonard, and Marco Morelli. Full practical handbook for
  cross-training multiple "modules" (physical, emotional, mental, spiritual, shadow-work)
  simultaneously, the applied-practice counterpart to Wilber's theoretical books.

### The Pocket Ken Wilber (2008) — E
- cover:
- Short pocket-format anthology of excerpts, publisher's companion to similar "Pocket"
  volumes on other authors. `[DRAFT]`

## 2010s–2020s

### The Integral Approach: A Short Introduction (2013) — eB
- cover:
- Ebook-only short introduction/overview essay. `[DRAFT]`

### The Fourth Turning: Imagining the Evolution of an Integral Buddhism (2014) — eB
- cover:
- Ebook applying AQAL to the future of Buddhism specifically, proposing what an
  explicitly "integral" fourth turning of the Dharma might look like.

### Wicked and Wise: How to Solve the World's Toughest Problems (2015) — CO
- cover:
- With Alan Watkins. Applies integral thinking to complex, "wicked" global problems
  (e.g., organizational and societal-scale challenges). `[DRAFT]`

### Integral Meditation: Mindfulness as a Way to Grow Up, Wake Up, and Show Up in Your Life (2016) — B
- cover:
- Distinguishes "growing up" (developmental stage), "waking up" (states of
  consciousness), "cleaning up" (shadow work), and "showing up" (quadrants/practice),
  and presents a meditation practice addressing all four simultaneously.

### The Religion of Tomorrow: A Vision for the Future of the Great Traditions (2017) — B
- cover:
- Wilber's longest and most systematic later work: a proposal for how the world's major
  religious traditions could integrate developmental psychology and post-metaphysical
  spirituality without losing their contemplative core.

### Trump and a Post-Truth World (2017) — B
- cover:
- Applies AQAL's developmental-stage model to contemporary American politics and the
  2016 election, reading the Trump phenomenon and "post-truth" discourse through the
  lens of stage regression/reaction. `[DRAFT]`

### Integral Politics (2018) — eB
- cover:
- Ebook extending the political application further. `[DRAFT — possibly overlaps with

### Finding Radical Wholeness: The Integral Path to Unity, Growth, and Delight (2024) — CO
- cover:
- With Corey W. deVos. Recent restatement of the full integral path aimed at general
  readers, framed around growth, waking up, and "showing up" in daily life. `[DRAFT]`
