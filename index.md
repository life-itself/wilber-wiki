# Wilber Wiki

### A concept-first index into Ken Wilber and Integral Theory

Reading Ken Wilber means constantly noticing "he's talking about *this* here" —
holons, postmodernism, complexity theory, Gaia, growth versus dominator hierarchies —
with no way to see everything he's said about one idea in one place. This wiki inverts
that: instead of a concept scattered across hundreds of pages, each core idea gets its
own page, pairing a synthesis of Wilber's view with the actual passages where he makes
the case, pulled from wherever in his work he makes it.

## The vision

A book's own index gets you a list of page numbers for a term; it doesn't synthesize
Wilber's view, and it definitely doesn't span multiple books. If you want to know "what
does Wilber actually think about complexity theory, and where does he say it," today
the answer is reread and remember, or go hunting.

The concept wiki collects the concept instead: a synthesized account of Wilber's view —
including a critical take, what's strong about it and what's contestable — sitting
directly alongside the passages where he argues it, across whatever works he's made the
case in. Not just his own named terms (holon, AQAL, the four quadrants) but, just as
often, **his take on a topic** — his critique of postmodernism, his read on complexity
theory's promise and limits, his account of growth versus dominator hierarchies. These
"Wilber on X" pages are frequently the more interesting entries, not an afterthought to
the canonical vocabulary.

This is an early pilot: seventeen concepts, sourced entirely from the full text of
*Sex, Ecology, Spirituality*, built to prove the shape of the thing before scaling out
to the rest of his work.
