# The Simple Feeling of Being

*Visionary, Spiritual, and Poetic Writings*

**Year:** 2004 · **Format:** Edited anthology

## Description

Anthology of Wilber's more lyrical/visionary passages drawn from across his books,
curated as a standalone contemplative reader.

## Relationship to earlier works

A collection of passages from earlier publications, emphasizing contemplative and inspirational writing rather than a new theoretical argument.

Source: [Publisher description](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/190456/the-simple-feeling-of-being-by-ken-wilber/).

The 2004 print edition was subtitled *Embracing Your True Nature*; the
subtitle above appears on later editions. The [library record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BA86606001)
identifies Mark Palmer and others as compilers/editors, while the
[editors' introduction](https://www.everand.com/book/770601386/The-Simple-Feeling-of-Being-Visionary-Spiritual-and-Poetic-Writings)
names all four. Wilber is the author of the selected writings.
