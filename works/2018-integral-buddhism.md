# Integral Buddhism

*And the Future of Spirituality*

**Year:** 2018 · **Format:** Book (previously published under another title)

## Description

Wilber applies the integral approach to Buddhism's possible future development,
using the tradition's successive turnings as a way to frame a further transformation.
See the [publisher's excerpt](https://www.shambhala.com/historical-introduction-excerpt-integral-buddhism/).
