## What to read

You do not need to read every introduction and restatement. If you want an accessible
first encounter, begin with *A Brief History of Everything*. For Wilber's mature
theory, our suggested route is *Sex, Ecology, Spirituality* → *Integral Psychology*
→ *Integral Spirituality*.

### An accessible entrance

#### [A Brief History of Everything](1996-a-brief-history-of-everything.md) · 1996

A conversational presentation of the broad integral synthesis. Choose it when you
want to understand what the framework is trying to do before committing to the much
longer *Sex, Ecology, Spirituality*. It is a gentler entrance to much of the same
territory, not another mandatory stop.

</div>

#### [The foundational synthesis](1995-sex-ecology-spirituality.md)

**Sex, Ecology, Spirituality · 1995**

The expansive statement of the four-quadrant synthesis: holons, evolution, culture,
modernity, and spiritual life. Read it for the argument and its context, not only for
the model's outline.

</div>

<a href="2000-integral-psychology.md" class="mx-auto block max-w-[190px] transition-transform duration-200 hover:-translate-y-1 focus-visible:outline focus-visible:outline-2 focus-visible:outline-offset-4">

</a>

#### [The psychological detail](2000-integral-psychology.md)

**Integral Psychology · 2000**

The clearest companion for levels, lines, states, and self-development. It revisits
the broad framework while making the developmental architecture more precise.

</div>

#### [The later turn](2006-integral-spirituality.md)

**Integral Spirituality · 2006**

The next step for post-metaphysics, methods and perspectives, and the relationship
between contemplative states and developmental stages. Read it once the earlier
framework is familiar.
