# Complete works catalog

For a selective route through the major books, start at the [Works hub](index.md) or
use the full [What to read, and why](reading-guide.md) guide. To trace changes in the
theory, see [The evolution of Wilber's thought](phases.md).

A chronological catalog of Wilber's books, edited collections, and selected digital
and audio publications. Dates refer to first publication unless an edition or
uncertainty is noted. This is not an inventory of every article, foreword, or recording.

★ marks an editorial selection of major works across theory, practice, and personal
writing; it does not mean that every starred book is essential to understanding the
theory. Labels beside titles identify reused material and related editions where
the relationship is known. A `draft` label means some publication details or the
description still need verification.

## Editions, collections, and gaps

A new title does not always mean a new work: *The Fourth Turning* became
*Integral Buddhism*, and *Trump and a Post-Truth World* became *A Post-Truth World*.
Their pages explain the evidence and where the extent of revision remains unknown.

The [Collected Works of Ken Wilber](https://www.shambhala.com/collected-works-of-ken-wilber.html)
repackages books and essays, with introductions and other material. Its volumes
should not be counted as eight additional independent theory books.

Bibliographic checks draw on publisher records, library catalogs, the
[Ken Wilber Fund catalog](https://www.kenwilberfund.org/books-by-ken-wilber/),
and [Integral World's bibliography](https://www.integralworld.net/bibliography.html).
The latter two are useful finding aids, but conflicting dates or credits require
edition-specific evidence. Wikipedia is a secondary source, not a primary source.

The catalog remains selective: standalone articles, forewords, many recordings,
and collaborative or pseudonymous publications are not comprehensively inventoried.
`Draft` entries retain explicit uncertainty rather than implying full verification.
Cover images are credited in the [cover sources](../assets/covers/SOURCES.md).
