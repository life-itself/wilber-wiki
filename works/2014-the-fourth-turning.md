# The Fourth Turning

*Imagining the Evolution of an Integral Buddhism*

**Year:** 2014 · **Format:** Ebook

## Description

Ebook applying AQAL to the future of Buddhism specifically, proposing what an
explicitly "integral" fourth turning of the Dharma might look like.

## Later title and related work

Published in print as [Integral Buddhism (2018)](2018-integral-buddhism.md).
The [library record](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB26588317) records its prior ebook
publication under this title. Do not count the two titles as separate reading requirements.

Wilber also calls *The Fourth Turning* a preliminary version of
[The Religion of Tomorrow](2017-the-religion-of-tomorrow.md) in an
[excerpt published by Shambhala](https://www.shambhala.com/future-religion-excerpt-ken-wilber/).
That is a development relationship, distinct from the retitling as *Integral Buddhism*.
