# First Principles and First Values

*Forty-Two Propositions on CosmoErotic Humanism, the Meta-Crisis, and the World to Come*

**Year:** 2024 (second edition 2025) · **Format:** Collaborative book under a pen name · **Byline:** David J. Temple

## Authorship

This book is credited to "David J. Temple", not to Wilber alone. The [David J. Temple profile](https://substack.com/@davidjtemple) of the Center for World Philosophy and Religion describes the name as "a pseudonym created for enabling ongoing collaborative authorship" between Marc Gafni, Zak Stein and Ken Wilber. Retail listings name all three alongside the byline, for example the [Amazon paperback listing](https://www.amazon.com/First-Principles-Values-Propositions-Cosmoerotic/dp/B0CS85WYVX). Nothing checked here says what share of the text is Wilber's, so the wiki lists it as a collaborative work, not as Wilber's own writing. It is not listed in the [Ken Wilber Fund catalog](https://www.kenwilberfund.org/books-by-ken-wilber/).

## Publication history

Published in 2024 by World Philosophy & Religion Press (paperback ISBN 9798989588909, [Open Library record](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL51618674M)). A second edition (ISBN 9798991743907, [Open Library record](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL57785018M)) is listed for March 2025. Open Library's publisher names for both records differ from the publisher's own credit. The title page has not been checked, so this page stays `draft`.

## Description

The book presents forty-two propositions for what its authors call "CosmoErotic Humanism", a proposed world philosophy that responds to the "meta-crisis". This wiki has not reviewed the text and makes no claim about how it relates to Wilber's integral theory.
